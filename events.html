Recorder: a 5-minute check (Cloudflare Worker). An event opens when Open-Meteo shows snowfall at Three Hills, Drumheller or Stettler (CMA 517) or Castor, Consort or Czar (CMA 518), when any 511 segment in CMA 517 or CMA 518 reports a condition other than Bare / No Report / Closed, or when Emcon trucks move with snow in the next-12-hour forecast. It closes when CMA 517 and CMA 518 are bare again, Emcon has been quiet for 60 min and no snow has fallen for 2 h. Truck “out” = first moved (> 250 m between checks) or newly appeared; “back” = stationary 30 min, stopped reporting, or left the feed; times are accurate to about 5 minutes.
