How this outlook works

Computed in your browser each time the page opens, when you return to it, and every 10 minutes. Rule-based scoring, no AI service. Every number is labelled with its source; anything missing shows "not enough data".

Weather: Open-Meteo hourly snowfall, temperature, wind gust (best-match blend plus GEM, GFS, ECMWF for model agreement); Environment Canada GeoMet HRDPS 3-h snowfall at the town; Environment Canada weather alerts.
Roads (CMA 517 only): 511 Alberta live conditions (via relay), Open-Meteo forecast snowfall at each segment's midpoint, past snow-event road history and Emcon deployments from the snow events recorder.

Road attention score = forecast 24-h snowfall at the segment (cm, Open-Meteo) + 2 if 511 currently not bare + 0.5 × past times the segment went non-bare in recorded events. Shown only when the score is above 0. "When" = first forecast hour with snowfall ≥ 0.1 cm at the segment.

Confidence: High = the models agree (24-h snowfall spread ≤ 1 cm, or all show none) and HRDPS agrees and there are 3+ recorded events. Medium = one of those fails. Low = models disagree by more than 3 cm, or two or more fail.
